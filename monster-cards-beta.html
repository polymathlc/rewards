How the beta works

  1. Students earn points by answering questions and completing learning tasks in the main system.
  2. A Genesis Booster costs 30 points and contains five monsters.
  3. The fifth card is always 3★ or better. Pity guarantees protect students from very long unlucky streaks.
  4. Duplicate cards unlock bronze, silver, gold and prism frames. They do not increase battle power.
  5. Students build a three-monster squad with a 12-star budget, so a thoughtful mixed-rarity team can defeat a team of rare cards.
  6. Arena battles award trophies only. This prevents students from using the game to generate points without learning.
Production security note: move pack generation and point deduction into a Firebase Cloud Function before public launch, so students cannot tamper with browser code.
